The Rubik School — sound credits
================================

assets/sfx/*.mp3 (except k-*.mp3)
  From "uisfx" 0.4.0 (soft theme), https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx
  Audio license: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

assets/sfx/k-*.mp3 (coin, step, land, impact, gem, confirm)
  Kenney sound packs (https://www.kenney.nl): Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio.
  License: Creative Commons Zero, CC0. Taken from the CC0 selection in
  "@netforge/asset-library-files" 0.2.0 (library/audio/sfx).

All files were converted to mono MP3 at 64 kbps. Bike bell, birds, waves and wind are
synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API (no files).

Shop pictures (v45): every bike, pet, outfit, hat, jewel, boat, chess set, rod and lure picture in the shop
is drawn by code in js/ext/v45-shop.js (original pixel art, no image files, no third-party art).
One master bike kit (wheel, tube, saddle, crank parts) builds all 15 bike designs; colour variants are generated in code.
